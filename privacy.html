Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Kebbi Bolt Station respects your privacy. This policy explains how our ride-booking service collects and uses information.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, phone number, Google account information, profile details, location, ride information and driver or vehicle documents where applicable.

How We Use Information

We use information to create accounts, connect passengers with drivers, calculate fares, manage rides, provide customer support, prevent fraud and improve our services.

Location Information

Location may be used to identify pickup and destination points, help drivers navigate to passengers and calculate journey distances. Location access should be controlled through your device settings.

Google Sign-In

If you sign in with Google, we receive information permitted by your Google account authorization, such as your email address and basic profile information.

Data Storage and Security

We use Supabase and related service providers to store and process information. We apply access controls intended to protect personal information.

Sharing Information

Relevant ride information may be shared between passengers and assigned drivers to facilitate transportation. We do not sell personal information.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information as needed to provide our services and meet legal obligations. Users may request account or personal data deletion through our support channels.

Children

Our service is not intended for children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the Kebbi Bolt Station support team through the application.
